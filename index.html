Who we are

New Bridge for Children International stands as a bridge between a child’s painful past and a hopeful future. We care for orphans, widows and young mothers in body, mind, heart and spirit, because every person is made in the image of God and has a God-given purpose.

Our vision

A world where every orphaned child, widow and young mother lives in safety, dignity and hope, restored and empowered through the love of Christ.

Our mission

To bridge children and families to a better future by providing Christ-centred education, loving homes and practical skills, and by building sustainable enterprises that keep our work strong and lasting.

Our core values

  • Christ-centredness
  • Compassion
  • Dignity and respect
  • Integrity and accountability
  • Protection
  • Empowerment
  • Excellence
  • Community and partnership
  • Sustainability

What we are building

Schools →

A primary school with eight classrooms for about 400 pupils, with priority places for orphans and the poorest children.

Children’s homes →

Four family-style cottages with caring house parents, giving 60 orphaned and vulnerable children a safe, loving home.

Skilling centres →

Tailoring, hairdressing, catering, baking and digital skills for about 120 widows and young mothers each year, with a crèche for their children.

Spiritual and health care

Discipleship, counselling, trauma healing, and health and nutrition checks woven through every programme.

Sustainability: farming and animal rearing

To avoid long-term dependence on donations, we are starting income-generating farms on the project land. They feed the children, supply our kitchens, create jobs and fund the vision. See the farm project →

Crops

Maize, beans, cassava, vegetables and bananas, with irrigation and storage.

Poultry

2,000 laying hens providing eggs for the children and for sale.

Dairy cattle

15 dairy cows supplying milk for the homes and the market.

Goats and pigs

Breeding stock whose offspring are shared with graduating widows.
